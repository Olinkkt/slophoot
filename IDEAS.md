# Ideas

Context: private project for one class (16 students + 1 teacher). Not competing with Kahoot on scale, so we win on being *ours*.

## Ranked ideas

1. **Meme layer** (later): meme images as question backgrounds, meme reactions on right/wrong, meme on the podium. Keep an image field on questions. ~1h per piece.
2. **Class-specific content**: questions about the teacher, school, running jokes; use player names in questions. Content only, no code.
3. **Funny end-of-game titles**: "Fastest wrong answer", "Always picks B", from per-player stats. ~2h.
4. **Teacher-friendly editing**: make a quiz fast on a phone; optional AI generation from lesson notes. ~1 day.
5. **Team mode**: 16 students become 4 teams of 4. ~2 days.

## Other angles

- Fairer scoring that doesn't only reward fast taps
- Distinct visual style (Kahoot looks kiddish)
- Async play (finish on your own time)
- Richer question types: draw on image, sort/rank, free text graded by AI, closest number wins (demo quiz #2 already covers some)

## Dropped (irrelevant at 17 people)

Player caps, accounts, self-hosting, scaling.
